Clipper Bot

Clipper Bot is a personal tool run by the creator of Jizz's Clips. It turns livestream moments into short vertical clips with captions, and posts the clips the creator approves to their own accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

How it works

  1. The tool finds highlight moments and edits them into short vertical clips with captions and credit to the streamer.
  2. Each clip is sent to a private Discord channel, where the creator approves or rejects it.
  3. Approved clips are posted to the creator's own connected accounts using each platform's official API.

TikTok

The tool connects only to the creator's own TikTok account through Login Kit. It uses the Content Posting API to post approved clips to that account, and reads that account's own videos to find and link to posted clips. It does not offer sign-in to other people and does not collect other users' data.
