Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Who this applies to

Clipper Bot is a personal tool used only by the creator of Jizz's Clips. The only user accounts it connects to are our own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else.

What data the tool uses

  • TikTok: our own account's basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar), our own public video list, and access tokens that let the tool post approved clips to our account.
  • Instagram and YouTube: access tokens for our own accounts, used only to upload approved clips.

How it is stored

Access tokens are stored only on our own computer, in a local file that is not shared or uploaded anywhere. We do not sell, share or use this data for advertising.

Other people's data

The tool does not collect, store or process personal data of other TikTok users.

Removing data

We can disconnect the tool at any time from each platform's app settings, which revokes its access, and we delete the stored tokens at the same time.

Contact

Message @jizzs.clips on TikTok or Instagram.
